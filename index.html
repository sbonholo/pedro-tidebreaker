    How to play

    Three steps

    1. Drive the boat.W or ↑ for throttle, S or ↓ to go astern, A and D to steer.Hold GAS to go and BACK to reverse. Use the arrows to steer.
    2. Pass between the buoys.Red to port, green to starboard. Follow the amber arrow and light beams to the next mark.
    3. Beat the clock.Two laps of twelve marks. Ran aground? Back off and steer clear, or press R to return to the last mark.
